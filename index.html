THE PHILIPPINE NATIONAL HEROES

HOW TO PLAY?

Press button number 1 and answer the question. If the player answers correctly, a video about the hero will be displayed. After watching the video, proceed to button number 2 and answer the next question. Continue this process until you reach button number 9, which is the finish line. You have 10 lives. For every incorrect answer, one life will be deducted.
